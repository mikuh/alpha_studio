# Theme Lifecycle Framework

## Theme Size

Classify size before lifecycle. Size determines whether the theme has enough time for building positions and profit realization.

| Size | Source | Typical Duration | Trading Implication |
|---|---|---:|---|
| National-level | State strategy, top-level policy, major industrial revolution, central ministry deployment | 1-12 weeks | Prefer trend core + central-capacity stock; allow staged entries in startup/fermentation |
| Industry-level | Sector policy, industry supply-demand shift, local policy, major company/industry event | 3-15 trading days | Trade the main line after confirmation; leader and central-capacity stock both matter |
| Stock-level | Individual announcement, rumor, niche catalyst, non-official information | 1-5 trading days | Treat as event/emotion trade; do not extrapolate into a sector trend without evidence |

## Required Daily Action Map

Every daily report must convert lifecycle analysis into operating instructions. Do not only describe a theme as "strong", "active", or "worth watching".

For the whole market, choose exactly one state:

| State | When to Use | Required Instruction |
|---|---|---|
| 完全不做 | Defensive market, weak data quality, no theme with catalyst + price confirmation | No new trades; review only |
| 只观察 | Market trial/defensive or themes are early, conflicting, or already high-risk | Track auction, breadth, leader, central-capacity; no open-at-open trade |
| 触发后轻仓试错 | Trial market or startup theme with credible catalyst but incomplete confirmation | Only test after listed triggers confirm |
| 只做主线核心 | Active/aggressive market with S/A fermentation theme | Only leader, central-capacity, or trend core that meets triggers |
| 持有/减仓优先 | Climax or retreat risk dominates | Existing confirmed core can be managed; no new back-row entries |

For every important theme, include these fields:
- `今日结论`: 可做 / 触发后做 / 只看不做 / 只持有不新开 / 减仓退出 / 禁止.
- `今日只做`: the exact role(s) allowed today, such as leader, central-capacity stock, trend core, or first credible laggard.
- `今日不做`: forbidden roles or setups, such as climax back-row, retreat averaging, rumor-only stock-level mapping, or weak central-capacity divergence.
- `触发再做`: auction, first 15-minute breadth, leader feedback, central-capacity confirmation, turnover, and source-confirmation conditions.
- `失效动作`: what makes the theme downgrade and what action follows.

If the evidence does not support a clear `今日只做`, write `今日不做，只观察...` and list the observation signals. A table that lists all themes without a do/not-do decision is incomplete.

## Theme Continuity Ledger

A daily theme system must have a start, follow-up, and end. Treat each actionable theme as an open research object until it is explicitly closed.

Required continuity states:

| State | Meaning | Carryover Instruction |
|---|---|---|
| 新进 | Theme first becomes actionable or enters the watch ledger | Define entry trigger, invalidation, expected review window |
| 继续 | Prior active theme remains supported by catalyst + price evidence | Existing research position can follow the stated holding protocol; no automatic next-open exit |
| 降级观察 | Evidence weakens but full exit trigger is not confirmed | Reduce active attention/weight; no new opening unless re-confirmed |
| 减仓退出 | Exit triggers are active or risk/reward deteriorates materially | Existing research position exits into open/strength/rebound plan; no new opening |
| 结束 | Theme cycle is closed for the current report horizon | Remove from active ledger after documenting close reason and final review date |
| 缺席复核 | Prior active theme is absent from today's hot-theme/news ranking | Do not delete; verify leader, breadth, central-capacity, catalyst continuity, and market regime before deciding continue or exit |

Continuity rules:
- Any theme marked `可做`, `触发后做`, `只做主线核心`, `只持有不新开`, or explicitly tracked yesterday must appear in today's continuity ledger.
- If the theme is no longer in today's top ranking, it must appear as `缺席复核` or `结束`; silence is not allowed.
- A `缺席复核` theme defaults to `无仓不新开；已有观察仓降级管理`. Upgrade back to `继续` only after direct evidence confirms.
- If a theme was already in `减仓退出` / `exit_review` and remains absent or fails direct evidence review on the next report, mark it `结束` and move it out of the active ledger.
- A theme can be removed from the active ledger only after a visible `结束` row with close reason, exit/hold result, and whether it remains on a low-frequency watchlist.
- For reports using research-position language, use `观察仓/研究仓/已有触发仓` rather than personalized portfolio wording unless the user explicitly asks for portfolio construction.

## Four Required Stages

Every theme must be in exactly one stage. Do not skip the stage call.

### 1. Startup

Signals:
- News appears but market consensus is low.
- 1-3 representative stocks move first.
- Internal breadth is narrow; central-capacity stock may only begin to turn.
- Limit-up count is small but purity is high.

Funding behavior:
- Smart money and early hot money test the theme.
- Institutions may build trend positions quietly for larger themes.

Strategy:
- Use small test positions only.
- Prefer the purest leader candidate or the first central-capacity stock turning up.
- Avoid low-purity concept mapping.
- Daily action: `触发后轻仓试错` for national/industry themes with credible catalyst; otherwise `只观察`. Do not buy at the open before auction/breadth confirmation.
- Holding protocol: default T+1 review, extend only when breadth expands and the central-capacity or trend core confirms.

### 2. Fermentation

Signals:
- Theme has repeated news or price confirmation.
- Limit-up count expands; first leader appears.
- Central-capacity stock confirms uptrend or strong volume.
- Internal roles become clear: leader, central-capacity, trend core, laggard.

Funding behavior:
- Hot money raises emotion leader height.
- Institutions add to fundamental or trend core if the narrative is real.

Strategy:
- Main operating window.
- Increase weight only after the market timing score allows risk.
- For institutional themes, buy/hold trend core. For hot-money themes, focus on leader, break-reversal, or first credible laggard.
- Daily action: `可做` or `触发后做` when market timing is active/trial and evidence confirms. This is the only stage where new main-line participation is normally allowed.
- Holding protocol: do not automatically sell at the next open. Hold while leader feedback, internal breadth, and central-capacity/trend core remain healthy; review at each close and after major catalysts.

### 3. Climax

Signals:
- Batch limit-ups, widespread media attention, high consensus.
- Back-row stocks accelerate; many one-word boards.
- Leader height is extreme; failed-board risk rises.
- Central-capacity stock may lag or show heavy volume.

Funding behavior:
- Early funds distribute into late followers.
- Back-row retail/late hot money chases.

Strategy:
- Do not initiate back-row positions.
- Hold only confirmed core if already positioned.
- Use strength to reduce exposure when diffusion quality worsens.
- Daily action: `只持有不新开` or `减仓退出`. New buys are limited to rare leader/core confirmation after intraday washout, and must be explicitly labeled high risk.
- Holding protocol: shorten to intraday/T+1 unless already holding confirmed core; reduce on back-row failure, leader open weakness, or central-capacity heavy-volume stall.

### 4. Retreat

Signals:
- Leader breaks with negative feedback.
- Failed-board rate rises; yesterday limit-up premium turns negative.
- Back-row stocks fall first; central-capacity stock fails to defend trend.
- New theme begins to compete for capital.

Funding behavior:
- Hot money exits quickly; institutions reduce or wait.
- Capital rotates to lower-position themes or defensive sectors.

Strategy:
- Reduce or exit.
- Only consider oversold rebound if the user explicitly asks for high-risk short-term tactics.
- Do not call a retreat "second startup" without a new catalyst and price confirmation.
- Daily action: `减仓退出` or `禁止`. Do not average down back-row or weak compensation names.
- Holding protocol: no default overnight hold. Re-entry requires a new catalyst plus leader and central-capacity confirmation.

## Duration and Holding Window Model

Use duration as an estimated remaining trading-day window, not a promise. Start from theme size and stage, then adjust daily.

Calibration rule:
- If a verified historical sample or local tracking dataset is available, compare current theme features with similar past themes and cite the sample window/source.
- If no dataset is available, label the window as `模型估计` and do not call it a backtest or big-data conclusion.
- Track realized duration each day: first credible evidence date, stage transitions, leader break date, central-capacity trend break date, and final retreat confirmation. Use this log to recalibrate future windows.

Base remaining-window guide:

| Size / Stage | Startup | Fermentation | Climax | Retreat |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| National-level | 5-20 trading days | 5-30 trading days | 1-5 trading days | 0-2 trading days |
| Industry-level | 2-7 trading days | 3-15 trading days | 1-3 trading days | 0-1 trading day |
| Stock-level | 1-3 trading days | 1-5 trading days | intraday-2 trading days | 0 trading days |

Adjustments:
- Institutional-led trend core: extend the upper bound when central-capacity stock trend, turnover quality, and fundamental driver remain intact.
- Hot-money-led leader: shorten the window when leader height is extreme, one-word boards dominate, or failed-board rate rises.
- Mixed theme: separate the trend-core holding window from the emotion-core holding window; do not use one duration for all roles.
- S-grade fermentation in active/aggressive market: upper bound can extend 20-30% if catalysts continue.
- B/C grade, defensive market, weak central-capacity, or rumor-only catalyst: lower the action to observation and shorten the window.
- Elapsed days matter. Compare `first_seen_days` with the base window and report the remaining window. If a theme is already near the upper bound, treat it as late-stage even if news is still positive.

Required output fields:
- `已运行`: trading days since first credible catalyst/price evidence.
- `预计剩余窗口`: range such as `2-5个交易日`, `盘中至T+1`, or `0-1个交易日`.
- `默认持有协议`: e.g. `趋势核心按收盘趋势复核，不自动次日开盘卖出`; `情绪龙头T+1复核，弱转强失败即退出`.
- `延长条件`: repeated catalyst, leader positive feedback, breadth expansion, central-capacity confirmation, turnover quality.
- `缩短/退出条件`: leader break, failed-board pressure, central-capacity divergence, catalyst denial, broad sentiment downgrade, new theme siphons liquidity.

## Cross-Cycle Rejection Rules

- Startup: do not size like a confirmed main line.
- Fermentation: do not keep treating core stocks as unproven if breadth and central-capacity confirmation exist.
- Climax: do not chase laggards because "the theme is strong".
- Retreat: do not average down back-row stocks.

## Stage Upgrade/Downgrade Triggers

Upgrade to fermentation:
- At least two days of theme evidence or one day of very strong national-level catalyst.
- Leader and internal breadth both improve.
- Central-capacity or trend core confirms.

Upgrade to climax:
- Batch limit-ups and widespread back-row acceleration.
- Theme concentration becomes too high and consensus is obvious.

Downgrade to retreat:
- Leader fails with negative feedback.
- Failed-board rate rises and central-capacity stock breaks trend.
- New theme absorbs liquidity.
