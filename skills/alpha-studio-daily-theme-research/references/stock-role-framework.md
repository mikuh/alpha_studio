# Stock Role Framework

Every key stock must have a role, reason, and industry-chain authenticity label. Do not list tickers without role logic.

## Industry-Chain Authenticity

Before assigning a durable role, verify whether the company is actually in the theme's industry chain.

Required labels:

| Label | Meaning | Role Permission |
|---|---|---|
| 产业链核心 | Products/services/revenue/orders are directly in the theme chain, supported by official or high-quality evidence | Can be central-capacity stock, trend core, or leader if price confirms |
| 产业链相关 | Adjacent supplier/customer/tooling/material/service link exists, but revenue weight or theme purity is partial | Can be role candidate; holding window shorter than core unless evidence improves |
| 概念映射 | Concept relation exists but direct business/revenue/order evidence is weak | Emotion/arbitrage only; cannot be called safe core |
| 蹭概念/弱相关 | Mostly concept tags, name association, weak互动易 answers, rumor, or very small exposure | Mark high risk; avoid as core and usually do not include in `今日只做` |
| 待验证 | Evidence unavailable or conflicting | Observation only until direct source confirms |

Evidence hierarchy:
1. Official disclosures: annual report, prospectus, exchange filing, company announcement, audited segment revenue, disclosed orders/customers/capacity.
2. Company official materials: official website product pages, investor relations records, verified product/customer descriptions.
3. High-quality secondary research: reputable broker/industry-chain reports, reliable financial media with concrete product/revenue/customer details.
4. Low-quality concept evidence: concept-board tags, social media, vague互动易 answers, unsourced screenshots, price涨停 alone.

Rules:
- Market behavior proves attention, not business relevance. A limit-up stock still needs separate business evidence.
- `中军`, `趋势核心`, and longer holding windows require `产业链核心` or strong `产业链相关`.
- `概念映射`, `蹭概念/弱相关`, and `待验证` stocks can only be emotion/arbitrage/observation candidates unless evidence upgrades.
- If company clarification denies or weakens the theme connection, downgrade to `蹭概念/弱相关` or exclude.
- The report must show the authenticity rating for each named stock and keep supporting evidence in role logic or a compact note.
- Show authenticity as a rating-only parenthetical suffix beside each stock name inside the original role matrix, e.g. `金海通（A）`, `华亚智能（D）`. Do not add a second line such as `产业链相关 / 证据等级A / 可作相关核心` inside the `标的` cell, do not use large badges, and do not replace `龙头 / 中军 / 趋势核心 / 补涨矩阵` with a standalone authenticity table.
- Add one short note below the table to explain ratings: `A/A- = official evidence and clear chain link; B/B+ = related but materiality still needs confirmation; C = concept mapping; D = weak/uncertain and cannot support central-capacity or trend-core role.`

## Roles

### Leader

Definition:
- The stock that defines the theme's emotional height and market attention.
- Usually earliest, strongest, most recognizable, or highest board.

Evidence:
- First to move or highest consecutive board.
- Strongest next-day premium.
- Break or board failure influences the whole theme.
- Narrative is pure and easy for the market to repeat.

Explain:
- Why this stock carries the theme's story.
- Why its price behavior controls theme sentiment.
- What would invalidate leadership.
- Holding review: usually auction/T+1 review for hot-money leaders; can extend only while leader feedback remains positive and theme breadth does not deteriorate.

### Central-Capacity Stock

Definition:
- The large-cap or high-liquidity stock that proves the theme has enough capacity for bigger funds.

Evidence:
- High turnover, large market cap, sector representativeness.
- Trend confirmation, volume expansion, institutional ownership or research coverage.
- Board can stay strong even if small leaders fluctuate.
- Must be `产业链核心` or strong `产业链相关`; a pure concept stock cannot be a central-capacity stock.

Explain:
- Why institutions can buy it.
- How it validates the theme's capacity.
- Whether it is rising trend, breakout, or defensive anchor.
- Holding review: can outlast the emotion leader if the trend, turnover quality, and fundamental driver remain intact; review by close and trend break rather than automatic next-open sale.

### Trend Core

Definition:
- Fundamental or industry-pure stock with sustained multi-day trend.

Evidence:
- Multi-day uptrend, moving average alignment, volume-price health.
- Revenue/earnings/order/supply-demand connection to the catalyst.
- Less dependent on consecutive boards.
- Must have official or high-quality business evidence. Concept-board mapping is not enough.

Explain:
- Fundamental link to the theme.
- Why holding time can be longer than pure event trades.
- Holding review: use moving trend, volume-price health, catalyst continuity, and central-capacity confirmation. Do not shorten to T+1 unless market sentiment or theme evidence deteriorates.

### Emotion Core

Definition:
- Stock with the strongest short-term emotional premium.

Evidence:
- Consecutive boards, break-reversal, weak-to-strong open, high attention.
- Drives hot-money behavior.
- May be concept-only, but must be labeled as such. Do not transfer emotion strength into business safety.

Explain:
- Why emotion funds recognize it.
- Which intraday behavior confirms or kills it.
- Holding review: normally intraday/T+1; extend only after weak-to-strong confirmation, board quality, and positive leader feedback.

### Laggard / Compensation Stock

Definition:
- Lower-position stock with similar narrative used after leader height opens space.

Evidence:
- Starts later, smaller gain, same concept mapping.
- Strength depends on leader not failing.
- If authenticity is only `概念映射` or weaker, treat as high-risk compensation only.

Explain:
- Why it is only compensation, not leader.
- Which leader/sector conditions must hold before it is tradeable.
- Holding review: no independent holding window. Exit/reduce when leader weakens or theme breadth fails.

### Arbitrage Stock

Definition:
- Related but weaker stock used for short-term spread capture.

Evidence:
- Partial concept relevance, lower purity, weaker liquidity or weaker trend.
- Usually `概念映射`, `蹭概念/弱相关`, or partial `产业链相关`; mark the limitation explicitly.

Explain:
- Why it is lower priority and what makes it unsafe.
- Holding review: intraday/T+1 only unless it upgrades into a clearer role with fresh evidence.

## Role Selection Rules

- One theme can have both an emotion leader and an institutional central-capacity stock.
- National-level and industry-level themes require a central-capacity stock check.
- Stock-level themes do not become main lines without breadth and capacity confirmation.
- If the central-capacity stock diverges negatively from small leaders, reduce theme grade.
- If leader fails but central-capacity stock holds trend, downgrade hot-money tactics but keep institutional watch.
- Always separate role-level holding windows. A theme may allow trend-core holding while forbidding new emotion/back-row trades.
- Never use a concept-only stock to prove a theme has institutional capacity.
- If all listed stocks under a theme are `概念映射` or weaker, downgrade the theme to stock-level/hot-money speculation unless other direct evidence exists.

## Stock-Level Probability Factors

Positive:
- Pure narrative mapping.
- Strong role clarity.
- Positive feedback from leader or central-capacity stock.
- Healthy volume and no extreme one-word exhaustion.
- Fundamental validation for institutional themes.
- `产业链核心` or strong `产业链相关` evidence from official/high-quality sources.

Negative:
- Back-row only.
- Concept is vague or rumor-based.
- Large prior gain with no new catalyst.
- Weak opening after theme news.
- Announcement clarification or shareholder reduction pressure.
- `概念映射`, `蹭概念/弱相关`, or `待验证` but being presented as core.
