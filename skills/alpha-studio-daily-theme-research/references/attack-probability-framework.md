# Capital Attack Probability Framework

Use this reference when the report must predict which A-share sectors/themes funds are most likely to attack today or over the next 1-3 trading days. The goal is not to list all good news. The goal is to identify the path with the best catalyst, liquidity, timing, and verification stack.

## Decision Funnel

Run the funnel in this order:

1. `隔夜全球线索`: US indices, sector leaders, ADRs, futures, rates, USD/CNH, risk appetite.
2. `国内催化层级`: official policy, ministry notices, exchange/company announcements, reliable media, industry data.
3. `昨日赚钱效应`: market regime, limit-up quality, leader height, failed-board pressure, yesterday premium.
4. `题材相对强度`: board breadth, relative sector strength, capital flow, turnover quality, first-seen date.
5. `角色承接`: leader feedback, central-capacity trend, trend-core liquidity, laggard diffusion quality.
6. `共识差`: strong catalyst but under-owned/under-discussed themes score higher than crowded climax back-row themes.
7. `竞价/盘中验证`: 9:15-9:25 auction, 9:30-9:45 breadth, 10:00 leader/central-capacity confirmation, afternoon rotation.

Stop the funnel when a required layer fails. A theme can remain a hypothesis, but it cannot become `可做` or `只做主线核心` unless price, role, and catalyst evidence confirm.

## Overnight Global Mapping

Map overnight moves into A-share attack routes:

| Input | Positive A-share Read-through | Negative / Caution Signal |
|---|---|---|
| Nasdaq / SOX / AI hardware leaders | Semiconductors, AI hardware, optical modules, PCB, servers, edge devices | Gap-up crowding if A-share names already climaxed |
| Tesla / EV / battery chain | Lithium battery, solid-state battery, robotaxi, charging, auto parts | Pure concept mapper if no domestic catalyst |
| US biotech / pharma | Innovative drugs, CXO, medical devices | Weak if domestic policy or licensing catalyst is absent |
| Oil / gas / coal / copper / gold | Resource cyclicals, precious metals, power equipment materials | Inflation/rate shock can hurt growth themes |
| USD/CNH, Treasury yields, VIX | Risk appetite, foreign-flow pressure, high-dividend defense vs growth attack | Strong dollar/rate spike can cap high-beta themes |
| China ADRs / Hang Seng Tech / A50 | Internet tech, brokers, consumption, index beta | ADR strength without A-share catalyst is only a secondary lead |

Treat global data as a lead, not proof. It upgrades a domestic theme only when it aligns with Chinese policy/news, prior A-share relative strength, and leader/central-capacity confirmation.

## Attack Route Types

Assign exactly one primary route:

| Route | When to Use | Execution Bias |
|---|---|---|
| `中军先行` | Institutional or mixed theme, high-liquidity core stock turns before back-row diffusion | Prefer central-capacity/trend core; use close/trend review |
| `龙头先行` | Hot-money theme, event catalyst, leader board height and emotion premium drive breadth | Prefer leader/emotion core only after auction and breadth confirm |
| `双核共振` | Leader and central-capacity both confirm, catalyst remains fresh, market regime active/aggressive | Core participation allowed; avoid low-purity back-row |
| `轮动防守` | Market trial/defensive, risk appetite weak, but resource/dividend/defensive themes have relative strength | Observe or use only confirmed trend core; no emotional back-row |
| `无主线观察` | Catalysts conflict, data quality poor, market regime weak, or themes are crowded/retreating | No new trade; build watchlist and wait for validation |

## Probability Factors

Use the scoring model as a guide, then sanity-check the result:

- `隔夜/全球共振`: Does the theme have external lead and domestic translation?
- `催化剂权威度`: Is the source official, company-filed, or reliable media?
- `催化剂新鲜度`: Is this new information, a repeated catalyst, or stale consensus?
- `相对强度`: Did the theme outperform before the news, during the news, or only after media attention peaked?
- `资金容量`: Can institutions or large funds deploy through liquid central-capacity stocks?
- `情绪载体`: Is there a clear leader with positive feedback and room to lift breadth?
- `共识差`: Is the theme under-positioned relative to catalyst strength?
- `竞争格局`: Is a stronger theme siphoning liquidity?
- `生命周期`: Startup/fermentation can be actionable; climax/retreat needs stricter proof or exit bias.

## Required Output

Every formal report must show:

- `今日资金进攻路径`: primary route, backup route, invalidation route.
- `今日进攻概率`: probability that this theme becomes or remains the primary attack direction today/next session.
- `为什么资金会进攻`: catalyst + overnight/global + sentiment + relative-strength logic.
- `只在什么条件下做`: auction, breadth, leader, central-capacity, turnover, and news-source confirmation.
- `买什么角色`: leader, central-capacity, trend core, emotion core, or none.
- `不买什么`: climax back-row, rumor-only mapper, weak laggard, retreat rebound unless explicitly requested.
- `失败怎么处理`: observe, downgrade, reduce/exit, or close tracking.

## Validation Times

Use these checkpoints:

- `盘前`: build hypothesis from previous close + overnight/global + morning news.
- `9:15-9:25`: leader premium, matched volume, group auction breadth, central-capacity expected open.
- `9:25`: run the auction-confirmation gate. Confirm, partially confirm, or deny the pre-market attack path before any 9:30 action plan.
- `9:30-9:45`: first wave breadth, failed-board pressure, whether funds attack one stock or a full chain.
- `10:00`: confirm main line or downgrade to rotation/observation.
- `13:30-14:30`: check afternoon capital return, high-position feedback, and whether a stronger theme siphons liquidity.
- `收盘`: update continuity ledger, lifecycle stage, remaining window, and next-day attack hypothesis.

## 9:25 Confirmation Logic

Use the 9:25 snapshot to answer one question: did real opening-auction money validate the pre-market path?

Upgrade only when all three align:
- `龙头`: premium and matched amount are strong enough to prove real demand, not just a symbolic high open.
- `中军/趋势核心`: opens with positive volume/price confirmation; institutional capacity is not diverging.
- `宽度`: the theme opens as a chain, not one isolated stock.

Common outcomes:

| Auction Pattern | Interpretation | Action |
|---|---|---|
| leader + central-capacity + breadth confirm | Main-line route confirmed | `主线确认`; wait for 9:30-9:45 secondary confirmation before active execution |
| leader strong, central/breadth incomplete | Hypothesis alive but not tradable at open | `触发后轻仓试错` or `假强转观察` |
| leader one-word/crowded, back-row weak | Consensus too high or liquidity trapped | `冲高兑现/不新开` |
| back-row high-open, leader/central weak | Weak diffusion, likely false strength | `竞价证伪` or `冲高兑现/不新开` |
| primary fails, backup opens as full chain | Rotate to backup route | Mark primary denied and backup as trigger-only until 9:30-9:45 confirms |

Never promote a single high-open stock into the day's main line without group breadth and central-capacity confirmation.
