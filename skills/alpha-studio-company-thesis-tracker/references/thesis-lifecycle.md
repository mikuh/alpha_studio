# Thesis lifecycle

- `building`: first version or key inputs remain incomplete.
- `strengthened`: new evidence materially increases support without changing the central premise.
- `unchanged`: new information is consistent but not decision-relevant.
- `weakened`: support deteriorates or a risk becomes more likely, but invalidation has not occurred.
- `invalidated`: an explicit invalidation condition is met or the central premise is no longer defensible.
- `archived`: coverage intentionally stops; this is not a bullish or bearish label.

Every version must cite evidence IDs, include at least one risk and invalidation condition, and state what changed. An `invalidated` Thesis remains in history; a later new premise starts a new chain instead of rewriting it.
